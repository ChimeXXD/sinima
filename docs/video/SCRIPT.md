# Launch video: script

PLAN week 3: "Video script (30–45 s): the evening problem, then the app answering it in both cities, then the Doomsday hook and the link." Made for X: plays silent on autoplay, so **every line is a caption burned into the picture**, no voice-over needed. Exported 16:9 and 4:5 (PLAN week 4). Pictures are the real app screens (D-66), never Marvel's logo, trailer or key art: the film's title in text and its TMDB poster as the app shows it (PLAN, "Care with the Doomsday hook").

Three scripts were written; **A is the one picked** (Chime, 20 Nov 2026: "do your recommendation"; D-164). B and C are kept below as the alternatives. The storyboard (Figma) comes next and is drawn from A.

Rules every caption keeps: 6 words or fewer where possible (read in under 2 s), the app's own words ("sinima", "Stay", "Not known yet"), nothing the app doesn't do. Numbers marked ⟨checked at launch⟩ are re-read from the live site the week of the launch.

## A. "The evening question" (40 s) · picked

The PLAN's structure as it stands: the problem, the app answering it, both cities, the hook, the link.

| Time | Caption (burned in) | Picture | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **6:40 PM. Lekki.** | A phone's lock screen; one message slides in: "film tonight?" (a plain bubble, no messaging app's look) | The bubble drops in; the clock ticks over to 6:41 |
| 3–7 s | **Which sinima? What time? Still on?** | Three grey browser tabs fanned out, each a blurred listing page (no real site shown) | Tabs flick past one after another, a little too fast: the hassle |
| 7–10 s | **Every sinima's times, in one place.** | The tabs fold into the Ticket S mark; "Sinima" wordmark | The S tears along its dotted line and opens into the app |
| 10–15 s | **What's starting soon near you** | Starting soon, Lekki: rows with countdown rings ("Starts in 25 min") | The ring sweeps; the number rolls (M6, as built) |
| 15–20 s | **Every showing, by time and area** | A film's page: times by area, the five date tiles | Scroll down the times; a tile is tapped, the list swaps |
| 20–24 s | **Stay after the credits? It says.** | The Stay or Go chip: "Stay: scene after the credits · TMDB" | The chip lands with its sticker pop |
| 24–28 s | **Book on the sinima's own site.** | The showing sheet: Book, Add to calendar | Sheet rises (M10); a tap on Book; a calendar event appears |
| 28–32 s | **Lagos and Abuja.** | The city stickers; Films in Lagos, then Films in Abuja. Under: "22 sinimas in Lagos · 5 in Abuja" ⟨checked at launch⟩ | The city sticker flips; the page slides across (M9) |
| 32–37 s | **Avengers: Doomsday opens Fri 18 Dec.** | Coming soon (or the film's page, if presales are up by then) with the TMDB poster as the app shows it; "Add 18 Dec to my calendar" | The poster card lifts; the date sticker stamps on |
| 37–40 s | **sinima.com.ng** · *Free. No sign-up.* | Ticket S mark, the address, the Home Screen icon | Hold; the eyes of the S settle. End |

**15-second cut** (PLAN Should, for reposts): 0–3 s, 7–10 s, 10–15 s, 32–37 s, 37–40 s, each trimmed by a third.

**Why A:** it is the order the PLAN asked for, so the approved plan needs no change; it sells the everyday use (any evening), so the video still works after Doomsday week and on the chimexd.work case study; and the Doomsday beat is timing only, which keeps the brand rule easy to hold. Its risk is length: 40 s is the top of the range, so the storyboard has to keep each beat to its seconds.

## C. "Countdown to Doomsday" (30 s)

Built around the date: opens on a big **18 Dec**, counts down, and shows Sinima as where you'll find your showing. "Stay after the credits?" gets the strongest beat (a Marvel film is the case Stay or Go was made for), answered honestly: "Not known yet" until a source names the scene.

| Time | Caption | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | **18 December.** | The date, huge, counting down |
| 4–9 s | **Where will you watch it?** | Coming soon with the poster |
| 9–18 s | **Every sinima's times, the minute they're up** | The film's page filling with times |
| 18–24 s | **Stay after the credits? We'll tell you.** | Stay or Go: "Not known yet" |
| 24–30 s | **sinima.com.ng** | The mark and address |

The most clickable in launch week and the shortest; but it leans on Marvel's film for the whole video (against the spirit of the brand rule), it says little about any other evening, and it dates on 19 Dec.
