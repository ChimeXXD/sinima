My pick

Departures board under a marquee

From the 89 references, these 12 fit together as one direction. Time leads everywhere, your cinemas get a band of their own, and the colours move from lagoon greens to black, off-white and marquee-bulb yellow. Under each section is a runner-up in case you'd rather go another way.

What the two screens would become

  1. Cinemas: a “Your cinemas” band at the top with filled hearts, in a different style from “All cinemas” below it. With no hearts yet, one friendly line that says what the heart does.
  2. Starting soon: no poster wall. Each showing is a text row like a departure board: film and cinema on the left, a big “12 min” on the right, the Book button under it. Started showings drop below a thin “now” line.
  3. Time filter: one row: 30 min · 1 hour · 2 hours · 4 hours · Today, with a line under it in plain words: “Starting between 18:40 and 20:40”.
  4. Colour: the Marquee palette. Dark mode is the hero; posters bring the other colours.

Your cinemas

The problem: your hearted cinemas looked like more of the same list. These three fix that with a distinct band, a mark that follows you, and a good empty state.

Runner-up: Rakuten, the same logo rows under a “Your Favorites” heading. Cheaper, but less distinct.

Starting within

The filter you asked for. One row of choices, a line that says what the filter means, and an honest answer when a window is empty.

Runner-up: Klook's short hour-range chips with the count on the button (“See 9 showings”).

Starting soon

Time first, posters gone. Read the next showing at arm's length, and see clearly what has already started.

Runner-up: FotMob, with the time in a column on the left instead of a countdown on the right.

Look and colour

Black, white and one bulb yellow, like a cinema marquee at night. Far from the greens, with big poster-style type for headings.

Runner-up: Foyer (cream, brown and cinema red), as on A24's cream ticket. Warmer and lighter; I'd mock it up as the second option if you want two to compare.

The Marquee palette on a real row

A Starting soon screen drawn in the palette, light and dark. Every pair passes its contrast check (text 17:1, labels on the yellow 13:1).
