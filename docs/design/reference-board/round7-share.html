Round 7: the share card

When someone shares a Sinima link in WhatsApp or on X, the chat shows a picture under it: the share card (also called a link preview). Today our links show no picture. This board is for designing it: one card per film (and per showing), plus one for the site itself for the launch post. 57 screens in four groups: your share picks so far, share cards other apps design, how links look once sent in a chat, and tickets and passes.

Most share cards here are tall, for stories. Ours will be wide (WhatsApp and X show 1.91 : 1, about 1200 × 630), so judge the style and what's on the card, not the shape. All are iPhone apps from Mobbin.

For each one, tap what you like and say exactly what in the box. Click a screenshot to zoom, drag over the parts you like, and type there too. Tap “Not for us” for anything to avoid; that helps as much as a like. Skip the ones you have no view on. Answers save in this browser; press Copy all answers when you're done and paste them to me.
