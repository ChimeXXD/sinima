01 · Since draft 2

What changed, and why the map is new

Draft 2 was one city with Cinemas as home. Since then the plan and the design moved on; this draft puts every decision since 12 Nov into one map.

D-62

Two cities

Lagos and Abuja at launch. Every page belongs to a city; the city is in the address and in the search bar.

D-55

Films is home

People arrive with a film in mind. The home page is Films; Cinemas and Starting soon are tabs.

D-61 · D-64

Four tabs

Films · Cinemas · Starting soon · Your cinemas, in the approved floating tab bar (phone, iPad) and pill nav (desktop).

D-65

First visit picks the city

"Where are you watching?" asks for location as the site opens. Lagos or Abuja is picked; anywhere else, Lagos.

D-64

Search across both cities

The city switch sits in the search bar: Lagos, Abuja or Both cities. Results are grouped by city.

D-50 · D-67

Format and logos

Find a film by format (IMAX, 4D) and area. Saved cinemas show their own logo as a sticker.

Unchanged rules that shape every page

Every time says where it came from and how fresh it is, in words · a showing that has started is never bookable · booking always goes to the cinema's own site · location never leaves the phone · a "Sample data" notice while saved pages power the site.

04 · Pop-ups and notices

Things that open over a page

These have no address of their own (except a shared showing link). On phone and iPad they are Ghost sheets from the bottom; on desktop, centred dialogs.

Showing sheet

Time, cinema, area, countdown; Book on the cinema's site; Add to calendar; Keep a countdown; freshness line. Started: no Book.

?showing=… (shareable)

City sheet

Lagos · Abuja · Both cities, each with its areas. Changes the city everywhere and saves it on the phone.

Search

Opens in place. Scope: Both cities · Lagos · Abuja. Matching films grouped by city; Enter shows all matches on Films.

Pickers

Area, day and format pickers on Cinemas (phone), each a small sheet (D-56).

Share

Share, Copy link, WhatsApp; the link opens on the exact showing in the right city.

Location prompt

The browser's own prompt on a first visit. Not ours to style; our page explains it.

Theme

Light and dark switch in the header (D-58, D-60). Dark is designed after light is approved.

Tab bar

Always on screen; the active tab names itself; a count sticker on Your cinemas.

Notices that can appear on any page (one at a time, the most serious)

  • "Sample data" while saved pages power the site
  • "Some times were read yesterday" when a source is a day old
  • "You're offline" with the age of the saved times
  • "We couldn't place you in Lagos or Abuja, so here's Lagos" after the first visit
  • "Abuja's cinemas are being added" until Abuja has data
  • "13 cinemas don't say their format" when a format is chosen

05 · Two cities

How the city works across the site

1

First visit to sinima-app.vercel.app

"Where are you watching?" opens and the browser asks for location.

2

In Lagos or Abuja

That city is picked. Continue → /lagos or /abuja.

3

Anywhere else, refused, or no answer

Lagos, with a line saying so. One tap on the city switches.

4

Next visits

The saved city opens straight away. The position itself is never kept or sent; only "lagos" or "abuja".

5

Switching

Tap the circled city in the title or the city chip in the search bar → City sheet. The page you are on reopens in the other city (a film page stays on the same film).

6

Both cities

For search only: results grouped "In Lagos" and "In Abuja". Browsing pages always show one city.

One address per city (O-16, proposed)

A link shared on X or WhatsApp always opens the right city, even for someone who has never picked one.

07 · Journeys

Four ways through, one ending: the cinema's site

A · First visit

"What's on?"

Opens Sinima → allows location → Abuja picked → What's on in Abuja → taps a poster → Film → taps 18:20 → sheet → Book on the cinema's site.

5 taps, no sign-up

B · Film in mind

"Where is Avengers: Doomsday showing?"

Search → types "Doom" → Both cities shows it in Lagos and Abuja → Film in Lagos → filters IMAX → picks a time → Book.

the launch-week journey

C · Right now

"What can I catch?"

Starting soon → Lekki → the 17:45 card says 15 min → Book. A card that has started shows "Started 3 min ago" and no Book.

D · A friend sent a link

"Coming to this one?"

Taps a WhatsApp link → opens on the showing, in the right city, sheet open → Add to calendar (30-min alert) or Book.

The film has started

The time greys out and says when it started; no Book.

The times are a day old

Said in words on the cinema and in the sheet; never hidden.

A cinema we can't read

Listed with "Times not available yet" and a link to its site.
